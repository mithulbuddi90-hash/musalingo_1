Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Musalingo ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when you use the Musalingo web app and PWA (the "Service").

This policy is provided in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and applicable rules.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide directly:

  • Account information: Name, email address (when you create an account or sign in via Google or email)
  • Profile information: Display name, avatar selection
  • Learning data: Courses added, lesson progress, XP, streaks, hearts, diamonds, completed lessons, practice history
  • User inputs: Text you type during lessons and AI practice features
  • Friend connections: Email addresses of friends you add

Data collected automatically:

  • Device and usage data: Browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, session identifiers, interaction patterns, and app logs
  • Local storage data: Settings, theme preferences, and learning progress stored in your browser

Data from third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: When you sign in with Google, Firebase processes your Google account information (name, email, profile picture) on our behalf
  • Google AdMob: For rewarded ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data. See Section 4 for details

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following specific purposes:

  • Provide the Service: To create your account, save your learning progress, and deliver course content
  • Personalize your experience: To show your progress, streaks, and course recommendations
  • AI-powered features: To generate practice questions and language exercises using Groq AI
  • Rewarded ads: To allow you to earn in-app rewards (hearts, diamonds) by voluntarily watching ads
  • Improve the Service: To analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and improve performance
  • Communicate with you: To respond to support requests and send essential service updates
  • Prevent abuse: To detect and prevent fraud, misuse, and security incidents
Legal basis: We process your data based on your consent (when you create an account or use AI features) and for the performance of our contract with you (to provide the Service). You may withdraw consent at any time.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Groq: AI-generated practice questions and language exercises. Text inputs are sent to Groq to generate responses. Groq Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data for ad serving and measurement. Google Privacy Policy
  • Netlify (or similar hosting): Website hosting and serverless functions

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob)

Musalingo displays optional rewarded video ads only. Ads are never shown automatically or as interruptions. You see an ad only when you actively click a button to earn a reward (such as +1 heart or +50 diamonds).

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad:

  • Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (AAID/IDFA) and ad interaction data
  • This data is used to serve the ad and measure its performance
  • AdMob may serve personalized ads based on your interests

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. If you do not wish to see any ads, simply do not click the reward buttons.

5. AI Processing (Groq)

When you use AI-powered features, your text inputs are sent to Groq's API to generate language exercises and feedback. Groq processes this data solely to generate responses and does not use it to train AI models. We do not store your AI conversation history on our servers beyond what is necessary to provide the immediate response.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Local storage: Your learning progress, settings, and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data remains on your device.

Firebase Firestore: When you sign in, your profile data (name, email, courses, progress, friends) is stored in Firebase Firestore. This data is retained as long as your account is active.

Retention period: We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service. If you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted from Firebase within 30 days. Local storage data remains on your device until you clear your browser data.

7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Grievance redressal: File a complaint about our data practices

To exercise these rights, contact us at the details below.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time by:

  • Going to Settings → Delete All Data in the app
  • Or emailing us at the address below

When you delete your account, we permanently delete all associated personal data from Firebase. Local storage data can be cleared through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Musalingo is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable security safeguards, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Access controls on our systems
  • Regular monitoring for unauthorized access
  • Data backups for continuity

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside India, including by our third-party service providers (Firebase, Groq, Netlify). We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
